Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: 16:9 landscape high-fidelity visual identity and listening-interface masterboard for a real web product
Primary request: Create an original visual direction comp for the Chinese product "声景" (Sound-scape), which turns one sentence into a layered, continuously evolving ambient sound world. This direction is called "Multiplane Dawn": each sound source behaves like a physical depth plane, so listening feels like the camera quietly entering a world. Make it elegant and contemporary, not nostalgic fantasy.
Composition/framing: A single 16:9 product surface, not a marketing poster, phone mockup or mood board. Build one panoramic rainy-night cabin soundscape from five visibly separate depth planes: near rain on glass, room resonance, fire, cabin shell, and distant thunder. The planes overlap and crop each other with clear parallax logic, occupying most of the canvas. A narrow left rail holds the product mark and current scene; a calm foreground strip holds play plus light/heavy and sparse/rich. A small side state shows a new scene generating without interrupting the current world. Avoid card grids.
Subject/content: Exact visible text, sparingly and verbatim: "声景"; "一句话，用声音描述世界"; "雷雨夜里，点燃篝火的木屋"; "播放中"; "生成中"; "轻"; "重"; "简"; "繁"; layer labels only "雨", "火", "木屋", "远雷". No invented claims, prices, testimonials or menu filler.
Visual system: Five matte painted planes with different focus, opacity and speed implied by their edges and occlusion; a restrained geometric icon made from three offset planes and one lit gap. Deep pine, moss, mist blue-gray, warm ember and dark umber. The visual language must scale to icon, banner and interface.
Materials/textures: refined matte gouache and translucent animation-cel haze, controlled paper tooth, no ornate decoration. The image should have authored illustration quality but restrained editorial discipline.
Typography: modern Chinese humanist sans with a quiet but distinctive silhouette; no antique serif, no fantasy lettering, no wide tracking.
Lighting/mood: rainy night seen from a warm but sparse interior; one ember accent; natural depth, no dramatic sun rays.
Constraints: original design only; the listener's task is clear in one glance; layers are functional, not decorative; no watermark.
Avoid: storybook cottage romance, Tolkien fantasy, vintage game UI, faux-gold borders, ornamental leaves, beige parchment, watercolor wash cliché, cute icons, glass card wall, lotus, bamboo, ink painting, waveform, equalizer, headphones, music notes, neon, purple gradient, generic meditation app, excessive rounded rectangles, illegible text.
