Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: 16:9 landscape high-fidelity visual identity and product-interface masterboard for a real web product
Primary request: Create an original, museum-grade visual direction comp for the Chinese product "声景" (Sound-scape), a system that turns one sentence into a layered, continuously evolving, editable ambient sound world. This direction is called "Darkroom Horizon". It must feel contemplative, elegant, exacting and premium, while visibly behaving like a real product rather than a meditation poster.
Composition/framing: A disciplined 16:9 landscape surface, not a photographed device mockup and not a mood board. Use one continuous soft horizon as the dominant structural move across the whole canvas. Reserve roughly the left quarter for a large app-icon specimen and the product name; let the central half become the immersive listening surface with the horizon, scene depth and current playback; use the right quarter and a quiet lower strip for only a few interface states, sound-layer relationships, abstract "light/heavy" and "sparse/rich" controls, and a reusable banner/pattern crop. Avoid grids of cards. The eye should read icon -> living sound world -> precise states.
Subject/content: Exact visible text, rendered sparingly and verbatim: "声景"; "一句话，用声音描述世界"; "雷雨夜里，点燃篝火的木屋"; "播放中"; "生成中"; "已就绪"; "轻"; "重"; "简"; "繁". Do not invent claims, prices, testimonials or extra navigation. The current soundscape keeps playing while a new one waits to switch.
Visual system: Silver-gelatin photographic depth reduced to atmosphere rather than literal scenery; a near-still horizon dividing air and a dark reflective field; subtle foreground, midground and far-field occlusion; one small asymmetrical warm signal marks a ready scene. The horizon must evolve into the icon geometry and the interface's state language. Quiet near-black carbon, pearl-silver, mineral sea-glass green and a restrained oxidized-coral signal.
Materials/textures: real silver-halide grain, smoked optical glass, low-reflection anodized aluminium, extremely controlled microtexture. Texture must look materially plausible, not an overlay filter.
Typography: restrained contemporary humanist grotesque with excellent Chinese typesetting, compact labels, no high-contrast fashion serif, no wide-tracked tiny uppercase costume.
Lighting/mood: low ambient evening light, precise darkroom luminosity, deep but readable shadows, calm without becoming sleepy or beige.
Constraints: original design only; strong scalable icon silhouette; no text baked into the icon; no stock component library; UI controls must be understandable; no watermark.
Avoid: lotus, bamboo, ink wash, ensō circle, zen stones, mountains, obvious ocean photo, waveform, equalizer bars, headphones, musical notes, play-triangle logo, glowing neon, purple or blue gradient, glassmorphism card wall, bokeh blobs, generic wellness app, cream-paper Japanese cliché, decorative SVG look, excessive rounded rectangles, fake 3D phone mockup, illegible microtext.
